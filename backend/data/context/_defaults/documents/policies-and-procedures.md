# Meridian Capital Partners — Policies and Procedures

## KYC (Know Your Customer) Policy
- KYC reviews must be refreshed every 12 months
- Approval required from: Compliance Officer (Sarah Chen)
- Escalation channel: #compliance-alerts

### Enhanced Due Diligence Triggers
Enhanced due diligence (EDD) is required when any of the following apply:
- High-risk jurisdiction (Iran, North Korea, Syria, Myanmar, Russia, Cayman Islands, Panama, BVI)
- PEP (Politically Exposed Person) status
- Adverse media hit
- Complex ownership structure
- High transaction volume inconsistent with profile

### KYC Document Requirements
- **Individual clients**: passport, proof of address, source of wealth declaration
- **Corporate clients**: corporate registration, financial statements, beneficial ownership documentation
- **Trust clients**: trust deed, financial statements, trustee identification

## AML (Anti-Money Laundering) Policy
- SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) filing threshold: $10,000 USD
- CTR (Currency Transaction Report) threshold: $10,000 USD
- Structuring detection window: 30 days
- Structuring pattern: 3 or more transactions just below threshold within the detection window
- Escalation channel: #compliance-alerts
- Review team: Compliance Team

## Sanctions Screening Policy
- Screening required for: all new clients and counterparties at onboarding
- Sanctions lists checked: OFAC SDN, UN Consolidated, EU Sanctions, UK Sanctions
- Rescreening frequency for high-risk clients: daily
- Rescreening frequency for standard clients: monthly
- On hit: Immediate notification to #compliance-alerts and Compliance Officer
- False positive review: Compliance team reviews within 24 hours

## Trading Policy
- Pre-clearance required for all employee trades: yes
- Blackout period: 14 days
- Personal trading approval channel: #compliance-alerts
- Restricted list check required before any employee trade

## Data Retention Policy
- Transaction records: 7 years
- KYC documents: 5 years after relationship ends
- Communications: 3 years
