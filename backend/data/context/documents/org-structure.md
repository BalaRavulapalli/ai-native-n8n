# Meridian Capital Partners — Organizational Structure

## Compliance Team
- Head: Sarah Chen, Chief Compliance Officer
- Slack channel: #compliance-alerts
- Team size: 8
- Responsibilities: KYC/AML program management, sanctions screening and escalation, regulatory reporting (SAR, CTR), employee trading oversight, regulatory exam preparation, policy development and enforcement

## Deal Team (Client Advisory)
- Head: Michael Torres, Managing Director, Client Advisory
- Slack channel: #deal-team
- Team size: 25
- Responsibilities: Client relationship management, pipeline management and deal execution, investment advisory, pitch preparation and client presentations, new client acquisition

## Operations Team
- Head: Lisa Park, Head of Operations
- Slack channel: #operations
- Team size: 30
- Responsibilities: Trade settlement and confirmation, daily reconciliation, client onboarding processing, wire transfer execution, account maintenance

## Risk Team
- Head: David Kim, Chief Risk Officer
- Slack channel: #risk-team
- Team size: 10
- Responsibilities: Market risk monitoring, credit risk assessment, operational risk management, portfolio concentration limits, stress testing

## Management
- Head: Robert Hayward, CEO
- Slack channel: #management
- Team size: 5
- Responsibilities: Strategic direction, cross-team coordination, client escalations, board reporting

## Approval Chains
- **New client onboarding**: Relationship Manager → Compliance Officer → Operations
- **High-risk transaction review**: Compliance Officer → Chief Risk Officer
- **SAR filing**: Compliance Analyst → Compliance Officer → BSA Officer
- **Policy exception**: Department Head → Compliance Officer → CEO
