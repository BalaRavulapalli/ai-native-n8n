# Meridian Capital Partners — Trading Policies and Procedures

## 1. Order Execution Policy

### 1.1 Best Execution
Meridian is committed to obtaining the best possible result for client trades, considering:
- Price and costs
- Speed and likelihood of execution
- Size and nature of the order
- Market conditions

### 1.2 Order Priority
Orders are executed in the following priority:
1. Client orders (in order received)
2. Firm proprietary trades (if any)
3. Employee personal trades (pre-cleared only)

### 1.3 Trade Allocation
When a block trade is executed for multiple clients:
- Allocation must be determined before the trade is placed
- Pro-rata allocation based on target allocation percentages
- No client may be systematically favored or disadvantaged
- Allocation records maintained for 7 years

## 2. Trade Reconciliation

### 2.1 Daily Reconciliation Process
The operations team performs daily trade reconciliation:
- Compare internal trade blotter against custodian records
- Compare cash positions across all accounts
- Identify and investigate all breaks (discrepancies)
- Breaks must be resolved within T+1 (one business day after trade date)

### 2.2 Break Resolution
- Breaks under $100: Auto-resolved with adjustment entry
- Breaks $100-$10,000: Operations analyst investigates and resolves
- Breaks over $10,000: Escalated to Head of Operations (Lisa Park) and reported to #operations
- All breaks logged in the reconciliation database

### 2.3 Failed Trades
A trade is considered failed if settlement does not occur by T+2:
- Operations contacts counterparty to determine cause
- If counterparty issue: document and escalate to RM
- If internal issue: immediate correction and root cause analysis
- Failed trade report sent to #operations daily at 4:00 PM ET
- Pattern of failed trades with same counterparty triggers review

## 3. Portfolio Management

### 3.1 Concentration Limits
- No single security may exceed 15% of a client's portfolio value
- No single sector may exceed 30% of a client's portfolio value
- Cash position should not exceed 20% for growth-oriented accounts
- Fixed income allocation should not fall below 20% for conservative accounts

### 3.2 Rebalancing
- Automatic rebalancing triggered when allocation drifts >5% from target
- Quarterly review of all portfolio allocations
- Tax-loss harvesting considered before year-end rebalancing
- Rebalancing trades flagged in system to distinguish from client-directed trades

### 3.3 Restricted List
The compliance team maintains a restricted list of securities that cannot be traded:
- Securities where Meridian has MNPI
- Securities of firm clients (potential conflicts)
- Securities under regulatory investigation
- List updated in real-time and checked during pre-trade compliance

## 4. Client Reporting

### 4.1 Regular Reports
- **Monthly**: Portfolio performance report, transaction summary, fee disclosure
- **Quarterly**: Comprehensive portfolio review, benchmark comparison, risk metrics
- **Annual**: Tax documentation (1099s), annual performance review, fee summary

### 4.2 Report Distribution
- Reports generated automatically on the 5th business day after period end
- Distributed via secure client portal and email (SendGrid)
- High-net-worth clients ($5M+): personalized commentary from RM
- Institutional clients: custom format per investment management agreement

## 5. Market Risk Monitoring

### 5.1 Daily Risk Metrics
The risk team monitors and reports daily:
- Value at Risk (VaR) at 95% and 99% confidence levels
- Stress test results against historical scenarios (2008 GFC, 2020 COVID, 2022 rate shock)
- Counterparty exposure by name and sector
- Liquidity metrics (days to liquidate positions)

### 5.2 Risk Alerts
Automatic alerts posted to #risk-team when:
- Portfolio VaR exceeds 2% of NAV
- Single counterparty exposure exceeds 10% of portfolio
- Liquidity score drops below threshold
- Intraday loss exceeds 1% on any portfolio

### 5.3 Risk Committee
- Meets weekly (Monday 8:00 AM ET)
- Chaired by CRO (David Kim)
- Reviews all risk breaches from prior week
- Approves exceptions to concentration limits
- Minutes distributed to #management
